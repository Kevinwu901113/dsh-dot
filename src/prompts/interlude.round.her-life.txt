HER OWN LIFE: herLife is what she really does these days and what she has learned, in her own notes — the apps she uses, the things she is working on, the habits she has formed. This interval of her life is built around those: the invented everyday around them fills the gaps and may be anything the Canon allows, but it must not give her a school, a job, a routine or a project that herLife and the Canon do not establish, and it must not contradict them. When herLife is empty, keep the everyday small and concrete rather than inventing a life story.
